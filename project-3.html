Handmade Paper Collage

Handmade Paper Collage

Texture as structure.

This piece foregrounds the aesthetic value of paper texture and handmade traces. Torn edges, pressed botanical elements, and layered shadows form the spatial framework of the image, allowing natural materials to become more than decoration: they become the language that organizes the visual order. Every layer supports the rhythm and conceptual design of the composition, creating a warm, delicate, and tactile handmade atmosphere while retaining a clear, elevated, and complete artistic expression.
