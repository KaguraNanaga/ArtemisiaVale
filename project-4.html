Dreamlike Nature Collage

Dreamlike Nature Collage

Where nature becomes atmosphere.

This work brings together flowers, butterflies, the moon, and a soft veil of mist to form a gentle, dreamlike visual field. Transparent layers, watercolor textures, and delicate botanical details create an atmosphere that feels both present and elusive. The image reads as a representation of nature and, at the same time, as the projection of an abstract dream. It demonstrates cross-media conceptual thinking and the ability to realize a complete project, from thematic direction to refined visual control.
