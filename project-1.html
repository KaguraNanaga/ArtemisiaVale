Floral Botanical Collage

Floral Botanical Collage

Layered botanical quietness.

This piece builds a light, poetic visual language through layered flowers, branches, leaves, and petals. Each natural element is carefully placed, transforming close observation of botanical forms into a refined abstract expression. Soft color, delicate edges, and generous negative space create depth and breath, forming a complete artistic system from concept and selection to composition and final execution. The work preserves the tactile quality of handmade collage while presenting a restrained, elegant, and expandable visual language.
