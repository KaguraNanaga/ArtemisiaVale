Minimal Nature Collage

Minimal Nature Collage

Restraint, balance, and silence.

This piece is built around minimal natural elements, using a restrained set of leaves, soft paper cutouts, and generous negative space to create a balanced and quiet composition. The palette remains within a muted natural range; the layers are subtle yet precise, emphasizing space, stillness, and visual breath. Conceptually, it shows how reduction and abstraction can lead to elegance, while also revealing a mature design process from conceptual selection and formal control to complete final presentation.
